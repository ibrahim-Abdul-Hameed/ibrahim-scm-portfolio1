04 · The Delivery

From run-up to boundary: the P2P cycle

Every purchase follows a disciplined end to end procurement process.Scroll to follow the ball down the pitch.

  1. 1

    Purchase Requisition

    Capture the need with quantity, specs and BOM.

  2. 2

    Sourcing & RFQ

    Find vendors, request quotes, compare pricing.

  3. 3

    Purchase Order

    Negotiate terms and release the PO.

  4. 4

    Goods Receipt

    Follow up on materials and receive against the PO.

  5. 5

    Invoice Verification

    Three-way match of PO, GR and invoice.

  6. 6

    Payment

    Hand off to FI, vendor paid, the over is complete.
